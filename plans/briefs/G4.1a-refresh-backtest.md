## Why

A member's `## Calibration (the blind backtest)` section in `decisions.md` is written once, by the
build. The growth loop never refreshes it. `voice refresh backtest <MEMBER_DIR>` re-measures the
current `decisions.md` blind on the growth tests kept since the last kept change, and drafts one
growth-loop change that replaces only that section. The person approves it, and `voice refresh
apply` tests and keeps or undoes it, as for any change from `propose`.

## What it does, in order

1. Reads `decisions.md` (`refresh::decisions`). Refuses, with `propose`'s own errors, while a change
   waits for an answer or an approved change waits to be applied (`refresh::pending`, made
   `pub(crate)`). Refuses with a plain error when `decisions.md` has no line exactly
   `## Calibration (the blind backtest)`.
2. **The tests:** every `new` growth test whose `created` is at or after the `at` of the newest
   measurement with outcome keep; every `new` test when nothing was ever kept. Sorted by `created`,
   then id. No cap: the dollar caps are the limit.
3. **The floor:** fewer than `voice_build::build::MIN_HELD` (30) tests drafts nothing and makes no
   model call. It prints one line saying how many there were.
4. **The answer step, blind:** in `voice_build::build::batches` of about 40, through the same
   `Asker` (privacy guard, `SOURCES`, model, caps) as `apply`. The prompt is `apply`'s answer prompt
   with its own opening line (each test's id, area, situation and options only, never `expected`).
   The system prompt `prompts/backtest.md` asks for an answer and a confidence (`high`, `medium`,
   `low`) per test. The reply is checked whole: the confidence is checked here, and the answers by
   `apply`'s own `check_answers`.
5. **The grade:** `apply`'s `grade_answers` (made `pub(crate)`) grades every answer: a choice test
   passes on its exact option, a rubric test by the rubric grader.
6. **The score:** a `voice_steps::score::Score` per area and tier (baseline, led and
   took-the-member left at zero: free text has no recommendation).
7. **The section:** the heading, the paragraph (`text::calibration`: how many tests, their date
   range, overall hits, the live list by `draft::live_categories`, the autonomy sentence), then the
   build's own table header and rows (`draft::calibration_rows`, split out of the build's
   `calibration()` so both use it). It replaces everything from the heading to the next `## `
   heading. When the result equals the current file, it drafts nothing and says so.
8. **The named tests (V-Q25 choice 1 A):** the 10 newest tests the backtest got right. None right
   drafts nothing and says so. `apply` is unchanged: on keep, only those 10 become in use; the
   rest stay `new` for `propose`.
9. **The checks before saving:** `draft::check_private` over the new `decisions.md`, and
   `refresh::check_words` over the summary, the reason and the new section, as `apply` will
   check them. Then the base is re-read (unchanged, as in `propose`) and the proposal is saved
   through the same helper `propose` uses (split out of `propose`, not copied).
10. The CLI prints the change as `refresh show` does, then the spend line. Flags: `--model`,
    `--daily-cap-usd`, `--call-cap-usd`, as `propose`, sharing `propose`'s set-up code.
